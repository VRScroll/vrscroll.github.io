VRScroll: Enhancing VR Sketching with a Dynamic Shape Display

Wen Ying and Seongkook Heo
University of Virginia
PaperVideoHighlightsCitation

A shape-changing physical surface follows virtual geometry to support precise sketching in VR.

Abstract

VRScroll is a shape-changing device that provides a physical drawing surface for precise sketching on virtual objects.

Seven independently controlled flaps approximate the local geometry of a virtual surface. The resulting physical contact constrains movement and provides friction and haptic feedback while drawing.

A user study found more precise tracing and clearer sketches with the dynamic shape display. The project also appeared as a CHI 2024 demonstration and an IEEE VR 2023 poster.

Video

A Physical Surface for Virtual Shapes

A flat tablet provides support but cannot match the changing geometry of non-planar virtual objects. VRScroll changes the angles between seven motor-controlled flaps to approximate a nearby virtual surface.

Study

The study compared surface support for tracing and sketching on virtual objects, measuring precision, stroke characteristics, and perceived experience.

Highlights

Matching physical support to virtual geometry improved tracing precision and sketch clarity. The results offer design implications for dynamic shape displays supporting precise on-surface interaction in VR.
